About us

Steve Strassmann, CEO

Steve is a 3x founder with a PhD in AI from MIT.
He has held senior engineering roles at Apple, Benchling, Kyruus, VMware, Orange, and others.
He served as CTO of Massachusetts, and has been EIR and a visiting scholar at Harvard Medical School and the Wyss Institute.

Michael Schrage, Founding Partner

Michael is a Lecturer at the MIT Sloan School of Management and Visiting Fellow in the Imperial College Department of Innovation and Entrepreneurship. One of the world's foremost authorities on managing innovation, advising Mars, P&G, Google, Intel, and IBM, and publishing regularly in Harvard Business Review, the Financial Times, and the Wall Street Journal.

Stan Kugell, Director

Stan was a researcher at the MIT AI Lab, Stanford AI Lab, and Xerox PARC, and the founder of three software companies. He’s a board director of a dozen corporations and nonprofits in US and Europe. He holds and and has held investor, director & C-suite roles in pharma, biotech, telecom, IT, and artificial intelligence.
